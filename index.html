Research

Research Experience and Ongoing Work

Water-Likeness Exposure for Sentinel-1 Water Segmentation

As a Research Assistant in the Department of CSE at SUST, investigated geographic training distribution shifts across the 65-scene Sentinel-1/Sentinel-2 (S1S2 Water) satellite benchmark. Proposed a scene-uniform exposure intervention improving site-macro IoU from 0.500 to 0.541 across 73 global benchmark sites over Empirical Risk Minimization (ERM). Designed evaluation frameworks across 3 paired random seeds to ensure experimental reproducibility and documented comparative error analyses across 5 baseline architectures.

Remote Sensing Sentinel-1 SAR Empirical Risk Minimization Hard-Negative Sampling

Preference Optimization of Protein Language Models

Engineered parameter-efficient fine-tuning pipelines using rank-8 and rank-16 LoRA on the 151-million-parameter ProGen2 autoregressive model. Tailored a homology-controlled benchmark from Learn2ThermDB containing 224,646 thermophile sequences and 49,399 pairs, demonstrating that Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) achieves a 76.56% win rate while Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) minimizes absolute amino-acid-conditioned negative log-likelihood to 2.6019. Manuscript under review at PLOS ONE.

Bioinformatics Protein Language Models PEFT / LoRA DPO / KTO

Earthquake Early Warning from Initial P-Waves

Curated a standardized benchmark of 7,318 South Asian seismic events using 7-second P-wave windows for rapid magnitude classification. Designed a Deep Attention Transformer achieving 81.56% adaptive accuracy with 4.8 ms real-time inference latency. Introduced the Adaptive Accuracy metric (±0.2 tolerance), proving that 60.3% of model errors concentrate at boundary interfaces (IEEE ICCIT 2025).

Deep Learning Seismic Signals Attention Transformers

Protein-Based Fish Species Classification

Introduced the first benchmark of 2,845 high-quality protein sequences across 9 native Bangladeshi fish species. Designed a hybrid MotifCNN-Transformer architecture with Terminal-Aware Positional Encoding, achieving 79.80% accuracy while being 42× smaller and 5× faster than ProtBERT to enable real-time GPU-free inference (IEEE QPAIN 2026).

Bioinformatics MotifCNN Terminal-Aware Encoding

Bangla Natural Language Processing & Transformers

Benchmarked transformer architectures across low-resource Bangla NLP datasets. Established new state-of-the-art benchmarks using BGE-M3 embeddings: achieving 99.49% accuracy in tense classification, 96.19% accuracy in formal vs. colloquial register distinction (BanglaShadhu), and 95.97% accuracy in regional dialect hate speech detection across Chittagong, Barishal, and Noakhali dialects (IEEE QPAIN 2026).
